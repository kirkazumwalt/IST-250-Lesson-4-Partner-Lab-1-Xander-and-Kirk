Performance Specifications

Engineering designed to push the Mustang further.

Performance

The Mustang GTD was made with the goal to deliver on exceptional track performance while also keeping the classic road-going Mustang. Its advanced suspension, aerodynamic systems, supercharged V8, and performance-focused drivetrain go hand and hand to make a unique Mustang.

Engine

5.2L Supercharged V8

Horsepower

815 HP

Torque

664 lb-ft

Transmission

8-Speed Dual-Clutch

Top Speed

202 MPH

Layout

Rear-Wheel Drive

Built Around Performance

The GTD goes beyond just engine power. Its engineering focuses on aerodynamics, handling, braking, and weight distribution to improve the performance around a track.

Performance Features

  • Supercharged V8 engine
  • Eight-speed dual-clutch transmission
  • Advanced suspension system
  • Performance braking system
  • Active aerodynamic technology
  • Carbon-fiber body components

Introducing the Mustang GTD

Race-Inspired Performance

The Mustang GTD brings Mustang styling and racing technology together in a road-going performance car.

The Ford Mustang GTD takes the Mustang in a very different direction. Inspired by racing, the GTD was developed with precision, performance, and aerodynamics being at the center of its design.

Its powerful V8 engine, aggressive bodywork, extensive use of lightweight materials, and advanced suspension separate the GTD from other Mustangs. Every part of the car is specifically made to help deliver excellent performance.

Although it has the Mustang name, the GTD carries ideas that are usually associated with high-performance racing machines. From its performance-focused interior to its aerodynamic body, it shows just how extreme its interpretation is of the Mustang.
